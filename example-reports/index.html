About this public package

All summary values and uncertainty bounds are retained, except for large Parquet data files and most individual radio/class-session plots are omitted due to space limitations. Links to substituted examples are explicitly marked, and the example images are watermarked. HBLL plots in the 2025 and two 2026 reports remain actual captured data, with 2026 captures in this location including native over the air captures for comparison. These reports are preliminary and may have errors.
